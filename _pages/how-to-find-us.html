🚗 Parking

From the DoES parking wiki: on-street parking on Kempston Street is free only after 11pm. During the day, paid on-street parking outside DoES is available for up to five hours. DoES encourages public transport whenever possible, because daytime city-centre parking can be difficult and depends on demand.

On-street, Kempston Street

  • £2.30 for 30 minutes
  • £4.40 for 1 hour
  • £6.50 for 2 hours
  • £10.60 for up to a maximum of 4 hours between 7am and 6pm
  • £10.60 for up to a maximum of 5 hours between 6pm and 11pm

Car parks within a short walk

  • Lambert Street — 69 spaces, £9.60/day, open 07:30–19:30, 2 minute walk
  • Kempston Street — 45 spaces, £9.60/day, open 07:30–19:30, 3 minute walk
  • Intelli-Park, Kempston Street — 32 spaces, £9.00/day, open 06:00–18:00
  • Burgess Street — 39 spaces, £9.60/day, open 07:30–19:30
  • Finch Place — 22 spaces, free, but only open 19:30–23:99. A contract car park during the day, and free for public use after 7:30pm on a weekday
  • Royal Liverpool Hospital — 500 spaces, £5.50 for 2 hours to £15.00 for 24 hours, open 24/7, 6 minute walk

For on-street parking controlled by Liverpool City Council (all but the Royal Liverpool Hospital), phone apps listed on the parking signs can be used instead of cash. There is a 20p booking charge, and the app warns you before the session expires.

Do not park in the Lidl car park beyond the time limit. They have CCTV monitoring the entrance and exit, which will detect longer stays.

Blue Badge: there are 4 bays at the top (east end) of Kempston Street. Blue Badge holders can also use the Kempston Street pay-and-display bays for free, for an unlimited time, not including loading bays. Display a valid badge.

The venue's guest Wi-Fi is DoESLiverpool, password decafbad00.

Questions about DoES can go to their public mailing list, groups.google.com/g/does-liverpool.

🚆 By train

The nearest train station is James Street (approx. 11-minute walk).

You can also use:

  • Liverpool Lime Street (mainline station – approx. 15-minute walk or short taxi ride)
  • Liverpool Central (approx. 15-minute walk)

From James Street:

Exit the station and head towards the waterfront → follow signs for Albert Dock → the Atlantic Pavilion is on the dockside. For evening meetups, use the side door (not the main arched entrance) — see On Arrival below.

🚌 By bus

Several buses stop near Albert Dock and the Pier Head area from Liverpool city centre.

You can plan your journey using:

  • Merseytravel Journey Planner
  • Google Maps

🚗 By car

The recommended car park is Royal Albert Dock Parking, directly adjacent to the venue at Royal Albert Dock, Gower St, Liverpool L3 4AS.

For more space, Liverpool ONE is the largest underground car park nearby and is a short walk from Albert Dock.

We recommend allowing extra time for parking during busy periods.

🚲 By bike

Albert Dock is easily accessible via Liverpool's waterfront cycle routes. Bike racks are available in the Albert Dock area.

🏢 On arrival

Important — evening entry:

Reception closes at 5pm, so the main Atlantic Pavilion entrance is not available for our meetups. Please use the side door marked in red on the photo below — an organiser will be there to let people in.

Side entrance — look for the door circled in red

Once inside, take the lift to Floor 4 for Acorn Insurance.

Arriving late? If there is no one at the side door when you arrive, contact an organiser via our Meetup group messages and we will come and let you in.
